# v0.3 development validation — 2026-10-26

## Scope and status

This record covers the first `0.3.0.dev0` milestone on branch `feat/v0.3.0`. It validates
controlled application-layer failures, bounded retries, worker-local idempotency and service
admission/backpressure behavior. It is not a v0.3 release record, a sustained-capacity campaign
or evidence for infrastructure failure containment.

The final macOS smoke campaigns were executed from clean commit
`5da64d6f477a1707ef531c5257c2e9b44a9360f0`. Their manifests consistently report
`git_dirty=false`; all planned rounds completed and no generator iterations were dropped. The
released v0.2 datasets and validation records were not relabeled or replaced.

## Functional recovery smoke

One measured execution per delegation boundary used the 1 KiB fixture, zero synthetic wait,
`after-commit`, two maximum attempts and worker-local idempotency. Each execution fans out to the
three specialists.

| Scenario | Offered / succeeded | Attempts | Retries | Kernel executions | Idempotency hits | Injected failures | Remote calls |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| local | 1 / 1 | 6 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 0 |
| HTTP | 1 / 1 | 6 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 6 |
| A2A | 1 / 1 | 6 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 6 |

All three results matched the deterministic business oracle. The first attempt for each
specialist executed the kernel and then returned the declared transient failure. The retry reused
the stable delegation ID and returned the cached result without duplicate kernel work. HTTP used
headers and A2A used the official public `Message.metadata` field for this application metadata.

## Clean macOS treatment campaigns

The three factorized smoke campaigns were rerun after committing the implementation. Reports were
generated only from these final datasets; earlier partial and dirty diagnostics were excluded.

| Campaign | Rounds | Offered | Succeeded | Errors | Dropped | Warmup passed / failed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Resilience smoke | 24 / 24 | 72 | 72 | 0 | 0 | 24 / 0 |
| Timeout smoke | 3 / 3 | 3 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 3 / 0 |
| Backpressure smoke | 18 / 18 | 450 | 39 | 411 | 0 | 9 / 9 |
| **Total** | **45 / 45** | **525** | **114** | **411** | **0** | **36 / 9** |

The resilience campaign recorded 378 specialist attempts, 162 retries, 243 kernel executions,
27 idempotency hits and 162 injected failures. Every supervisor execution recovered and matched
the business oracle. The extra 27 kernel executions relative to the 216 logical specialist calls
come from treatments where retry occurs without an idempotency cache hit; the result exposes that
duplicate-work cost instead of hiding it.

The timeout campaign recorded 18 specialist attempts, nine retries, nine kernel executions and
nine injected delays. `delay-first` does not enter the business kernel on the timed-out first
attempt, so recovery completed without hidden late kernel work. The matrix uses a one-second
attempt deadline, 2,000 ms injected delay and 1,500 ms retry backoff. Transport startup/readiness
deadlines remain separate from this measured application timeout.

The backpressure campaign intentionally saturated admission limits. It recorded 565 specialist
attempts, 117 kernel executions and 448 overload rejections; 39 of 450 supervisor requests
completed successfully. An execution can fan out to three specialist delegations, so attempt and
rejection counters are not expected to equal request-level error counts. The 411 errors and nine
warmup failures are treatment outcomes, not harness failures. All 18 rounds completed, no offered
iteration was dropped, and the raw status values remain in the evidence.

These short loopback campaigns validate treatment semantics and the experiment path. Their
latencies must not be presented as portable capacity results or as a general ranking of local,
HTTP and A2A boundaries. Generator and systems under test shared the macOS host.

## Validated failure semantics

Automated tests additionally cover:

- deterministic fail-before-execution, delayed first attempt and fail-after-commit treatments;
- per-attempt timeout distinct from the overall supervisor deadline;
- retry exhaustion with completed-attempt metrics retained in the failure sample;
- recovery with and without idempotency, exposing duplicate kernel work explicitly;
- permanent conflict when one idempotency key is reused with a different canonical task hash;
- bounded cache capacity and TTL configuration;
- immediate overload rejection and bounded queue timeout;
- expected treatment failures during warmup recorded as `warmup_failed`;
- unexpected transport/setup failures still aborting warmup;
- schema-v3 integrity, report generation and compatibility with v1/v2 result files.

## Not validated in this record

- The 1,920-round resilience, 120-round timeout and 270-round backpressure matrices.
- Sustained saturation with an external isolated load generator.
- Container CPU/memory interference and resource-isolation treatments were outside this first
  milestone; their later smoke validation is recorded separately in
  [v0.3-resource-isolation.md](v0.3-resource-isolation.md).
- Process, proxy, packet, machine or cross-host failure injection.
- Durable cross-worker idempotency or A2A durable task recovery/polling.
- Collector/exporter failure matrices and lost-span accounting.
